# Gold, Land, and Banks

Each player starts with 600 gold and 0 land.

- Gain 10 gold per second.
- Gain up to 10 more gold per second, multiplied by your share of the Top middle line. A 60% share pays 6 gold per second.
- Gain up to 10 land per second, multiplied by your share of the Bottom middle line.
- Pay upkeep each second: one percent of the gold cost of each living unit you control.

How that share is measured is on [[The Middle Line]]. Spend land on [[Towns]]. Unit costs are on [[Units]].

Each player has three banks, locked at the start. A bank costs a starting price plus extra gold for each bank already open. Banks become available at the start of the match, at 2 minutes, and at 4 minutes. Each new bank adds more gold per second than the last: the first adds 3, the second adds 6, and the third adds 9. Buying a bank is on [[Quick Start]].

Alternates cost land as well as gold. Elite (yellow) alternates cost 20% of their gold price in land; light (white) alternates cost 10%. Both ratios are tunable. Basic units cost gold only.
