# Melee

Footprints that overlap on the same row, or on an adjacent row, are in melee. Footprint reach is on [[The Map]]. Both sides strike with their melee speed and melee damage.

A charging unit gets its charge bonus on those hits. It also gets its flank bonus when it is on the adjacent row, or on the same row and behind its target. Only the charger gains the flank bonus. Grenadiers do not take extra damage from a flank. Dragoons use a larger flank bonus. Lancers use a normal flank bonus and a larger charge bonus. Charge, and what you can order in melee, are on [[Orders]]. Those unit differences are on [[Units]].

How the bonuses combine is on [[Damage]]. Units do not shoot a unit that is already in melee. See [[Shooting]].
