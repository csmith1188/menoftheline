# Melee

Two units are in melee when their bodies meet on the same row, or on an adjacent row. Contact uses each unit's body plus a short melee slack past the hard [[The Map|footprint]], so chargers can lock without walking into each other. Both sides strike with their melee speed and melee damage. While locked, a unit holds still unless it is Retreating. See [[Orders]].

A charging unit gets its charge bonus on those hits. It also gets its flank bonus when it is on the adjacent row, or on the same row and behind its target. Flank uses melee reach along the lane (footprint plus that same slack). Only the charger gains the flank bonus. Grenadiers do not take extra damage from a flank. Dragoons use a larger flank bonus. Lancers use a normal flank bonus and a larger charge bonus. Charge, and what you can order in melee, are on [[Orders]]. Those unit differences are on [[Units]].

How the bonuses combine is on [[Damage]]. Units do not shoot a unit that is already in melee, and a brief gap after contact still counts as locked for shooting. See [[Shooting]].
