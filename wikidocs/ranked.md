# Ranked Play

Ranked is a ticketed 1v1 ladder. Casual, custom lobbies, bot games, and training do **not** change ranked MMR or officer ranks.

## MMR

Every account starts at **1000** MMR. Wins and losses against other ranked players move your rating with an Elo system. Provisional players (before placements finish) use larger rating swings. Surrenders and abandonments after tickets are charged count as losses.

Your numerical MMR stays hidden until you finish placements.

## Placements

New officers begin as **Ensign**. After **10** completed ranked matches, your MMR is revealed and you enter the daily officer commission roll.

While placing, the dossier shows **Commission Pending: N/10 Battles**. Matchmaking still uses your hidden MMR.

## Officer ranks

Once a day (UTC), active officers who finished placements and played ranked within the last **30** days are ordered by MMR and given Napoleonic titles by percentile:

| Rank | Share |
|---|---:|
| General | Top 1% |
| Lieutenant General | Next 3% |
| Major General | Next 6% |
| Colonel | Next 15% |
| Lieutenant Colonel | Next 25% |
| Major | Next 25% |
| Captain | Next 15% |
| Lieutenant | Next 7% |
| Ensign | Bottom 3% |

With fewer than **100** eligible officers, only **Major**, **Lieutenant Colonel**, and **Colonel** are used (25% / 50% / 25%). Provisional players stay Ensign and are not in that pool.

Inactive officers keep their MMR and last title but leave the percentile pool until they play again. Your **highest rank** is remembered.
